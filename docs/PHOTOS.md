# Photos for SecurePass

The site works without photos (coloured placeholders are shown). To use real pictures, save them as **JPEG**, about 1600 x 1200 px, under 400 KB each, with exactly these file names. Use photos you are allowed to use (Unsplash, Pexels, or your own) and keep the credit list in the README.
