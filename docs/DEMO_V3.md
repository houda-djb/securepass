# Demo script for v3.0 (about 8 minutes)

1. **Home.** Show the dinar prices, the season tabs ("Where to go this autumn") and the top trips from Algiers. Switch to EUR and back to DZD.
2. **Countries.** Open Countries, then one country: best months, trip styles, cities and fares.
3. **Plan by month.** Choose July and "Beach" and show the suggestions change.
4. **Search.** Search Algiers to Istanbul. Point out the boarding-pass result and the filters.
5. **Book.** Log in, choose the flight, enter passenger names, pick BaridiMob, confirm. Choose seats.
6. **E-ticket.** Open the e-ticket and print it. Check the confirmation email in the log.
7. **Security.** Show a wrong password lockout, the forgot-password flow and two-step login.
8. **Admin.** Open the security dashboard, then Reports; download the CSV and open the audit log entry for the export.
9. **Operations.** Show `/healthz`, the Render dashboard and the green CI run on PostgreSQL.

Say clearly that payments are a demo and that no real card data exists anywhere in the system.
